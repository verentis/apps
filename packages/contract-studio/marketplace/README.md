# Contract Studio

Draft a commercial agreement point by point. Write original wording, insert a specific firm precedent revision, and attach pinpoint citations from installed Legal Research catalogues. The editor keeps sources, overrides and confidential review notes beside the wording.

Submit the rendered agreement for human review. Approval freezes the wording and provenance; issue writes clean TXT and Office-editable Flat OpenDocument (`.fodt`) files and a separate internal JSON source schedule. Copy the FODT for negotiation rather than changing the approved original. No external research service or law pack is required to draft and issue.

**Pilot boundary:** synthetic material in isolated workspaces. This app does not enforce ethical walls, authenticate an approver’s qualifications, determine whether legislation applies, or redistribute statutory text.
