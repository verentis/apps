# Legal Research

Search the country packs installed in your workspace by citation, instrument or topic. Select a result to open its pinned source. Where a provision has a recorded history, compare two dates to see whether the source changed.

Install **South Africa Law**, **Namibia Law**, or both to choose your coverage. Legal Research itself includes no country index. If you previously used the bundled South African index, install South Africa Law to keep using it. Searches stay in your workspace; external sources open only when you follow a link.
