# Legal Research

Find South African legal authorities by citation, instrument or topic. Select a result to open its pinned provision and source publication. For provisions with a recorded history, compare two dates to see whether the source changed.

Browse 195 South African instruments and selected pinpoint references to POPIA, the Constitution, the Companies Act, the Legal Practice Act and the Consumer Protection Act. Coverage is shown on every result. This is a citation index, not a complete law library or a finding that a provision applies. Sources open only when you follow a link; your searches stay in the workspace.
